Single-medium effects are often easier to model than media environments, but they may also be less faithful to how people actually encounter information.

My working intuition is that a **repertoire** is not merely a larger number of media channels. It is a patterned arrangement of attention, habit, access and substitution.

## Addendum · 18 August 2026

A useful distinction may be between *diversity* and *configuration*. Diversity captures breadth; configuration captures structure. The two should not be treated as interchangeable.
