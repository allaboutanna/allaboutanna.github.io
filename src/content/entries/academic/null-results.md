---
title: "Null results can sharpen an argument"
module: academic
date: 2026-08-03
summary: "Non-significance is sometimes a boundary condition, not an absence of knowledge."
tags: ["statistics", "methodology"]
order: 3
---

A null result becomes informative when it rules out a theoretically plausible relationship under a clearly specified design.

The interpretive task is not to decorate non-significance, but to ask what range of explanations becomes less credible after the result.
