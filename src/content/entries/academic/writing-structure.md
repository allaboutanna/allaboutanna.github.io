---
title: "Structure before elegance"
module: academic
date: 2026-07-28
summary: "Good academic prose usually begins with argument structure, not sentence polish."
tags: ["academic-writing", "research"]
order: 4
---

Before polishing sentences, I try to identify the job of every paragraph: establish a problem, delimit an explanation, introduce evidence, or state an implication.

Elegant sentences cannot rescue a section whose argumentative sequence is unclear.
