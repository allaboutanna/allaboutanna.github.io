title: "What makes a research question worth keeping?"
module: academic
date: 2026-08-08
summary: "A short reflection on questions that survive after the first attractive result disappears."

order: 2
---

A strong research question should remain interesting even if the preferred hypothesis fails.

That sounds obvious, but it is a useful test. If the entire project collapses when one coefficient changes sign, the project may have been built around an expected result rather than a durable question.
